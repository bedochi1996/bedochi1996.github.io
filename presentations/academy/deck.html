Threat Hunting Academy

A working threat-hunting platform built around INE's eCTHP path: 55 source-cited lessons, a 643-question bank, a server-graded 60-question paper, and eleven lab scenarios that launch real containers running real analyst tools over evidence you can verify by hash.

It is not finished, not deployed and not open to anyone. Its own launch definition records 3 of 8 invite-gate items met, one half-met and four open. Everything in this deck was photographed from the running stack on 19 September 2026.

The problem

Threat hunting is taught as theory and tested as trivia

A hunter's job is to form a hypothesis, go to the evidence, and come back with a determination they can defend. Almost none of the training market asks anyone to do that.

  • Courses hand out slides; the learner never opens a capture.
  • Exams ask which answer is correct, not what the evidence shows.
  • Labs are recorded videos, or a sandbox with the answer already on screen.
  • Arabic-language material is a translation layer over the same theory.

This platform's premise: if a claim cannot be checked, it does not ship. Applied to the curriculum, to the exam, to the evidence in every lab — and to this deck.

Deliberately absent

  • No public sign-up, no payments, no organisations.
  • No mobile application.
  • No AI tutor and no generative grading.
  • No live malware; no outbound access from any lab.
  • No Kali box and no Windows desktop — INE's own path hands the learner neither, and a test fails if either returns.

Not deployed

The platform runs on one laptop. Hosting is chosen and unbought. Nothing here has an address anyone can reach, and nothing in this deck says otherwise.
